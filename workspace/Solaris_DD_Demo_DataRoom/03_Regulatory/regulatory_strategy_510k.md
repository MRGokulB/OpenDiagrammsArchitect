# Regulatory Strategy - Solaris Arc v2.3

Document status: Founder approved.
Demo status: Synthetic company data with public FDA context.
Last updated: 2026-05-27.

## Proposed pathway

Management currently plans a 510(k) premarket notification for Solaris Arc v2.3. FDA describes a 510(k) as a premarket submission used to demonstrate that a device is substantially equivalent to a legally marketed device.

## Proposed intended use

The Solaris Arc microcatheter is intended for controlled delivery of diagnostic, embolic, or therapeutic agents into peripheral and neurovascular anatomy under physician direction. Final wording remains under regulatory review.

## Predicate strategy

The regulatory team is preparing a comparison matrix against legally marketed neurovascular microcatheters and delivery catheters. The comparison covers intended use, material composition, dimensions, coating, torque response, radiopacity, sterilization method, shelf life, packaging, and performance testing.

## Testing plan

- Dimensional verification
- Tensile and bond strength testing
- Kink resistance
- Trackability and pushability
- Particulate evaluation
- Biocompatibility per ISO 10993 test plan
- Sterilization validation
- Packaging integrity
- Simulated-use testing in tortuous vascular models
- Shelf-life validation

## Open regulatory risks

1. FDA may request additional clinical evidence if proposed indication wording is broader than predicate language.
2. Coating and particulate testing must support risk controls for vascular use.
3. Software traceability in Solaris CloudLab must remain separate from claims about device treatment performance.
4. Human factors validation may be required if labeling and workflow controls are viewed as materially different from predicate use.
