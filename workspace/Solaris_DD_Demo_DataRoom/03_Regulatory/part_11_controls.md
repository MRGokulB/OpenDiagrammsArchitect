# Electronic Records and Part 11 Control Summary

Document status: Founder approved.
Demo status: Synthetic company data with public FDA context.

## Scope

Solaris uses an electronic quality management system for design controls, CAPA, supplier qualification, training records, and approval workflows. The company is implementing controls aligned with 21 CFR Part 11 for electronic records and electronic signatures.

## Current controls

- Unique user accounts for all eQMS users
- Role-based access controls for document creation, approval, and release
- Audit trails for document revisions and approval events
- Electronic signature meaning attached to approval events
- Training assignments linked to released procedures
- Quarterly access reviews
- Backup and retention controls for regulated records
