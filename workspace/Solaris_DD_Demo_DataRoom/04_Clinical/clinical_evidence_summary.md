# Clinical Evidence Summary - Solaris Arc v2.3

Document status: Founder approved.
Demo status: Synthetic company data.
Last updated: 2026-05-27.

## Evidence generated to date

Solaris has completed bench testing, simulated-use testing, and an OUS observational clinical registry. The current evidence package is intended to support risk assessment, pivotal-study design, and investor diligence. It is not a claim of U.S. market clearance.

## OUS observational registry

Sites: 5.
Cases enrolled: 62.
Procedural technical success: 94.1%.
Mean device navigation time: 11.8 minutes.
Device-related serious adverse events: 0 adjudicated.
All-cause serious adverse events within 30 days: 3, none adjudicated as device-related.
Protocol deviations: 4 minor deviations.

## Simulated-use program

Total simulated cases: 184.
Physician evaluators: 23.
Successful target access: 178 cases.
Reported device kinking events: 2.
Reported hub or bond failure: 0.

## Pivotal study concept

Study type: Prospective, multicenter, single-arm pivotal study.
Planned enrollment: 140 subjects.
Primary endpoint: Technical success without device-related major adverse event through 30 days.
Secondary endpoints: Navigation time, successful agent delivery, device deficiency rate, and physician usability score.
Target sites: 12 U.S. clinical centers.

## Clinical risks

The registry is not randomized and is not powered to detect rare events. The pivotal study must confirm performance in U.S. clinical practice, demonstrate consistent operator training, and show that device-related adverse-event rates remain within the risk threshold accepted by the clinical advisory board.
