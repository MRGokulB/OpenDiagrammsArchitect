## Company snapshot

Solaris Endovascular, Inc. is a Delaware C-corporation developing a minimally invasive neurovascular access and embolic-delivery platform for interventional radiology and stroke-adjacent procedures. The company is preparing for a Series B financing to complete a U.S. pivotal study, expand quality systems, and submit a 510(k) premarket notification package.

Solaris has completed 184 simulated-use cases and 62 first-in-human observational cases outside the United States. The company reports a 94.1% technical success rate across the observational cohort, with no device-related serious adverse events adjudicated by the independent clinical events committee.

## Investment highlights

- Product: Solaris Arc microcatheter platform with torque-stable delivery and low-profile distal navigation.
- Stage: Design freeze complete; pivotal study protocol drafted; U.S. IDE strategy under review.
- Regulatory path: Management expects a 510(k) pathway if substantial equivalence can be established against cleared neurovascular delivery catheters. FDA describes 510(k) as a premarket submission used to demonstrate substantial equivalence to a legally marketed device.
- Revenue model: Direct sales to U.S. comprehensive stroke centers after clearance; distributor partnerships outside the United States.
- 2025 revenue: $3.84 million from OUS evaluation units, training kits, and distributor minimums.
- 2025 gross margin: 61.8%.
- 2026 forecast revenue: $7.95 million, dependent on EU distributor expansion and continued clinical training demand.
- Current cash: $5.62 million as of 2026-03-31.
- Estimated runway: 13.5 months at the current operating plan.

## Key diligence risks

Regulatory risk: The 510(k) pathway depends on predicate-device alignment and final indication wording. If FDA requests additional clinical evidence or a De Novo pathway, commercialization timing could shift by 9 to 18 months.

Clinical risk: The observational clinical dataset is promising but not randomized and not powered for rare adverse events. The pivotal study must validate navigability, procedural success, and device-related adverse-event rates.

Customer concentration risk: Three distributor and evaluation accounts represented 58% of 2025 recognized revenue. This concentration should be monitored against the 2026 direct-sales plan.

Manufacturing risk: The company currently relies on one qualified braided-shaft supplier and one secondary supplier in qualification. A supplier delay could affect pivotal-study inventory.

Compliance risk: Solaris is implementing Part 11-aligned eQMS controls, HIPAA administrative safeguards, and SOC 2 evidence collection. Audit readiness is targeted for Q4 2026.
