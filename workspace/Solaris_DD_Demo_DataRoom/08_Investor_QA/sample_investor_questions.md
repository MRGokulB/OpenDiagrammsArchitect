# Sample Investor Questions for Demo

Use these prompts after ingestion to show citation-backed answers.

## Regulatory

What is the proposed FDA pathway for Solaris Arc and what are the main risks?

Which documents support the 510(k) timeline?

Does Solaris have Part 11 controls in place?

## Financial

What was 2025 revenue and gross margin?

How long is the company's runway as of March 31, 2026?

How concentrated is revenue by customer?

## Clinical

What evidence supports technical success?

What are the limitations of the OUS registry?

How many sites are planned for the pivotal study?

## Commercial and access control

Which customers have minimum commitments in 2026?

Can a general investor view the cap table?

Which investors look ready for a meeting?
