## Mission

Solaris Endovascular develops minimally invasive catheter systems intended to improve precision, physician control, and access reliability in complex neurovascular procedures.

## Product family

Solaris Arc: Microcatheter delivery platform for tortuous neurovascular anatomy.
Solaris Track: Single-use access support wire and training kit.
Solaris CloudLab: Software workflow for device traceability, lot release evidence, and clinical case documentation.
