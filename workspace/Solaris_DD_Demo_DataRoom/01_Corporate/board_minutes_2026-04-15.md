## Attendees

Maya Patel, Evan Marks, Lina Chen, HelioSeed Ventures representative, Northstar Medical Fund representative, MedVector Strategic observer, outside counsel.

## Resolutions approved

1. The board approved preparation for a Series B financing of up to $18.0 million.
2. The board approved the pivotal clinical study budget, subject to final CRO contract review.
3. The board authorized management to begin FDA pre-submission preparation for the Solaris Arc v2.3 510(k) strategy.

## Risk discussion

Regulatory pathway: The board discussed whether Solaris Arc v2.3 should proceed through a 510(k) submission or whether indication wording may require additional evidence. Management agreed to produce a predicate comparison matrix by 2026-06-30.

Customer concentration: The board noted that three customers contributed 58% of 2025 revenue. Management stated that 2026 pipeline includes six additional evaluation sites and two distributor expansions.

Data room readiness: The board requested a founder-approved DD report with citation-backed answers before opening investor access.
