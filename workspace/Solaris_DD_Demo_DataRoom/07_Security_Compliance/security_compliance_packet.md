## Security posture

Solaris maintains a cloud-hosted quality and clinical evidence environment with role-based access controls, MFA, audit logs, endpoint management, and encrypted backups. The security program is designed to support investor diligence, clinical evidence protection, and regulated-record integrity.

## Compliance alignment

HIPAA: Solaris treats identifiable clinical data as restricted and stores only the minimum necessary clinical metadata in the diligence environment. Business associate agreements are required when protected health information is handled by vendors.

SOC 2: Solaris is collecting evidence for a SOC 2 Type I readiness review targeted for Q4 2026. Controls include access reviews, change management, vendor risk management, incident response, and backup testing.

21 CFR Part 11: Electronic signatures and electronic records in the eQMS are being validated through workflow testing, audit-trail review, and training controls.

FedRAMP-aligned: Solaris is not FedRAMP authorized. The company maps selected controls to FedRAMP Moderate as a future-readiness exercise for potential public-sector or academic medical center requirements.

## Active security controls

- MFA enforced for workforce cloud accounts
- SSO enabled for eQMS and clinical document repository
- Quarterly user access reviews
- Centralized logging for administrator activity
- Encrypted backups with monthly restore test
- Vendor security review for critical vendors
- Incident response tabletop planned for 2026-10-15
